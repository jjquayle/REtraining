The Programme

One monthly pre-recorded training video released in Google Classroom, accompanied by curated readings and resources. 10 sessions per year, covering all domains of the LSLS certification framework.

£160 per F-form

Entry Requirements

Is This Programme Right for You?

This programme is designed for qualified professionals already working with children with hearing loss who wish to work towards LSLS certification.

Professional Qualification

A recognised degree in audiology, speech-language pathology, education of the deaf, or another field approved by AG Bell.

Current Registration

Active registration or licensure with your relevant professional body (e.g. HCPC, RCSLT, BAA in the UK).

Clinical Experience

Demonstrated experience working with children with hearing loss and their families in a clinical or educational setting.

Access to Clinical Population

Ongoing access to children with hearing loss and their families to accumulate the required supervised practice hours.

Technology Requirements

Reliable internet connection, computer or tablet with camera and microphone, and permission to record families on your caseload.

Upfront Payment

First two months' fees are required to secure your place. Thereafter, payments are collected monthly via standing order.
